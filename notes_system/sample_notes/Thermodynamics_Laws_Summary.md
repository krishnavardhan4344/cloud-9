# Thermodynamics: Laws Summary

## Zeroth law
If A and B are each in thermal equilibrium with C, then A and B are in equilibrium. This defines temperature.

## First law
Energy is conserved: **dU = dQ - dW**.
- Isothermal ideal gas: dU = 0, so Q = W.
- Adiabatic: Q = 0, so dU = -W.

## Second law
- Kelvin-Planck: no engine can convert all heat into work in a cycle.
- Clausius: heat does not flow from cold to hot by itself.
- Carnot efficiency: `eta = 1 - T_cold / T_hot` (temperatures in kelvin).

## Third law
Entropy of a perfect crystal approaches zero as T approaches 0 K.

### Quick check
An engine runs between 600 K and 300 K. Maximum efficiency = 1 - 300/600 = 50%.
